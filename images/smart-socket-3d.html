The cable to buy

One USB-C to USB-C cable
  • Both ends USB-C. A cheap, plain charging cable is ideal.
  • "USB 2.0", rated 3 A (often sold as "60 W"). Not USB 3.x, USB4, Thunderbolt, 100 W, 240 W or 5 A: those have many thin wires and a chip in the plug.
  • Any length: you cut it about 25 cm from one plug and keep that plug end.
Not a USB-A to USB-C cableThat kind has no CC wire, and its plug has a resistor built in that would fight the socket's own signalling. Both ends must be USB-C.

Wires inside the cut cable

WireGoes to
RedPad +5V
BlackPad GND (the bare braid too)
CCPad CC. Colour varies (often yellow, blue or white)
DataNot used: cut short and cover with tape

Colours are not standard, so test each wire: plug the cable into the USB-C socket board (IDX 48), set the meter to beep, and touch a wire end and the board's VBUS, GND, CC1/CC2 and D+/D− pads. Every working USB-C to USB-C cable has VBUS, GND and CC.

If your phone has a Lightning port instead of USB-C, say so before buying: this design needs a USB-C phone end.
